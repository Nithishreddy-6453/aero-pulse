# Problem Alignment

AeroPulse turns live Pan-India air quality telemetry into daily, low-friction household action:

- **Zero Hardcoded AQI:** All AQI, PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, O₃, and 24-hour Clean Window metrics stream directly from Open-Meteo (`Open-Meteo / CPCB Grid`) with an exact IST sync timestamp in the navbar.
- **Actionable "Why & Impact":** Every recommendation across Today, Room Audit, and Commute & Impact quantifies both personal exposure reduction and CO₂ impact.
- **Tailored Indian Context:** Supports 4 household personas, LPG gas-stove & asthma modifiers, Rupee-tiered indoor fixes (`Free`, `DIY`, `Smart`), and local/intercity Leaflet + OSRM commute guidance.
